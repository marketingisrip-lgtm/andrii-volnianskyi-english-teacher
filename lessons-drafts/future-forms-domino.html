How to play

A domino has two halves. The left half answers the box before it. The right half is a new situation.

Evidence words are the words that tell you which form to use, like already booked, look at or I promise. Tap them first.

Wrong card? You get a hint. After two wrong tries the right card is shown.

Read each finished sentence aloud.
